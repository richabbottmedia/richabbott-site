About Rich Abbott

Rich Abbott is a Canadian author, wealth psychology educator, and publisher based in Manitoba. A former pastor with a B.A. in theology from Briercrest College, he writes Christian nonfiction and wealth psychology books under his own name and prophetic speculative fiction under the pen name R.G. Abbott.

His writing is shaped by years of pastoral ministry, personal experience with financial loss and recovery, and a conviction that most people are stuck not because of what they lack — but because of what they believe.
